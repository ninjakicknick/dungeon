# Artwork — The Crooked Crown

Created for this project using the built-in image-generation tool. No external art or asset service is used at runtime. Original generated images were encoded to WebP at quality 91; all referenced files live in this directory. Earlier Underchapel and Clint Bellanger assets remain in the repository but are not bundled into this slice.

## Shared direction

Create one finished 16:9 landscape first-person illustrated dungeon game background, 1536x864 or larger. Full bleed image ONLY, no text, no UI, no border, no character hands. Original whimsical dark fantasy board-game dungeon aesthetic: hand-painted gouache-like shapes, strong ink contours, sculpted chunky forms, rich jewel colors, dramatic warm/cool light, beautifully readable geometry, inviting mystery. Think lavish 1990s fantasy adventure illustration interpreted with modern clean color, NOT photorealistic, NOT pixel art, NOT gray archaeological ruins. Eye-level human camera physically inside room, clear walkable foreground, exits visible and meaningful. Confident shapes and restrained detail. All requested coordinate locations approximate image percentages. 

## Scene briefs

### fountain

A round ochre and terracotta chamber dominated by a large jade GREEN FROG fountain at center (50%,53%). It wears a small crooked copper crown and spits a thin stream into a circular turquoise pool with broad walkable sandstone rim (50%,70%). Gold coins visible under clear water. Warm coral flagstones. At LEFT wall (16%,44%) a tall open rectangular doorway shows a crimson corridor lined with imposing bronze armor. At RIGHT wall (86%,47%) a squat rounded tunnel shows vivid teal mushrooms and violet glow. Amber lanterns, cozy magical dungeon, room feels like a crossroads. No other doors, no religious motifs.

### armor

A long lavish CRIMSON corridor of oversized empty bronze suits of armor, different whimsical horned helmets, heavy broad shoulders, four along each side, dark red wall panels and black-and-cream diamond floor. One foreground suit at left (25%,46%) holds a long halberd upright and has a hinged narrow visor. Center far open doorway (50%,45%) reveals a tiny narrow stone bridge and violet chasm. First-person looking straight down corridor toward bridge. Warm copper lights, theatrical shadows. The nearest helmet should be large enough to notice a change, but no glowing eyes.

### armor-back

The reverse view in a crimson dungeon armor corridor: first-person standing at its far end looking BACK toward a round open doorway at center (50%,44%) through which a recognizable jade frog fountain and turquoise basin in golden terracotta chamber can be seen. Oversized empty bronze suits of armor with whimsical horned helmets on both sides, black and cream diamond floor, dark crimson wall panels. Warm copper lanterns. This is reverse geography, not a mirrored picture. No other doorways.

### bridge

First-person standing on a wide near ledge before a very narrow gracefully arched ivory stone bridge across a vast near-black purple chasm. Strong steep depth, no water. Bridge runs from foreground bottom center (50%,85%) to an ornate round doorway at center far wall (50%,37%). Beyond far doorway is a warm copper bell and a huge serene carved stone face, small in distance. Near LEFT edge (14%,70%) is low stone parapet and clear viewpoint to peer into the abyss. Violet basalt cavern with tiny amber windows improbably embedded in distant cliffs, some dark. Overhead chains disappear into darkness. Indigo, plum, pale ivory, a tiny warm destination. Wonderful enormous dungeon void; human eye-level, not aerial.

### bridge-back

Reverse viewpoint from far end of narrow ivory arched bridge over a huge purple-black chasm, looking BACK across bridge to warm crimson armor corridor visible through center doorway (50%,39%), tiny bronze armor silhouettes and diamond floor inside. Near foreground broad platform in bronze-lit rocks, low parapet left, bridge narrows toward far doorway. Vast violet basalt cliffs, impossible tiny amber windows in distant rock, hanging dark chains. Deep indigo and plum with warm copper destination. Physically standing on far landing, not overhead.

### shaft

A dedicated first-person looking DOWN view over rough ivory stone parapet at bottom edge into an impossibly deep cylindrical purple black dungeon shaft. Strong dizzying perspective, many small squared doorways and stair fragments along violet basalt walls converging toward a tiny warm amber light deep below at center (51%,73%). One small amber lit rectangular window in right shaft wall (73%,37%), another dark window left (26%,52%). A hanging iron chain descends from top right out of view. A narrow edge of the ivory bridge crosses top left. Mysterious depth, rich plum shadow. No creatures, no hands, no UI. A place to stand still and listen.

### mushroom

Low sprawling underground mushroom chamber, first-person. Enormous luminous peach-orange mushroom caps at left and tiny mint turquoise mushrooms in clusters, lush mossy carpet, saturated teal, emerald, rose and lavender, warm spores. A curled root makes a natural comfortable seat at front left (23%,72%). An open terracotta arch at LEFT BACK (18%,38%) reveals a tiny jade frog fountain in warm amber room. A higher narrow rocky passage at RIGHT (83%,36%) shows a copper bell and part of an enormous sandstone carved face in amber light. Meandering clear stepping stone path links the exits; no river, no extra doors. Largest central cap center (50%,45%) with dangling luminous gills. Cozy luminous fungal grotto, strongly distinct room.

### bell

First-person in a tall amber sandstone chamber facing an enormous sleepy serene stone FACE carved into entire back wall. Forehead at 15% height, huge closed eyelids, wide CLOSED mouth like a straight stone seam at center (50%,63%). A single broad worn stone step in front of mouth. A large antique copper bell hangs on chain in foreground upper center (50%,37%), thin pull rope down to (50%,72%). At left (15%,49%) open arch shows violet chasm and ivory bridge. At right (86%,53%) small low arch glows teal from mushrooms beyond. Red and gold patterned floor, warm saffron sandstone, monumental storybook scale, low human camera. Giant face feels absurd and strangely friendly, no religious imagery, no treasure visible.

### tea

A tiny absurdly cozy hidden dungeon room with plum velvet walls, low golden ceiling and ONE red velvet armchair angled toward a small round table with a steaming porcelain teacup and an uncut lemon. Table at (58%,64%), chair at (38%,57%). Behind table, on right, tiny circular window (78%,31%) looks into purple chasm with amber windows. One copper reading lamp. At extreme left (10%,48%) the open doorway has unmistakable giant stone lips forming its jambs, beyond faint bell chamber glow. Rich burgundy, orange gold, plum, intimate warm composition, first-person at threshold. Delightful incongruous discovery, no people, no text, no other doors.

## Aligned state edits

- `bell-open.webp`: edit of `bell.webp`, keeping camera, side exits, bell, rope and lighting; the giant mouth opens to a warm burgundy sitting room. Crossfaded in place after ringing.
- `armor-awake.webp`: edit of `armor.webp`, raising only the nearest left helmet visor to show an empty black interior. Timed environmental state.
- `tea.webp`: final edit of the original sitting-room illustration to show the warm bell chamber through the left doorway while preserving the purple shaft in the right window.

The generated doorway glimpses establish landmarks, not pixel-identical views of adjacent sets. Reverse illustrations are separately authored rather than horizontally flipped. `shaft.webp` is a dedicated downward composition, not a zoom of the bridge. Close views elsewhere use the same image and clamped crops.
